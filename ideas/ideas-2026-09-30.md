## Authority building

N1 [S leadership | text]
Hook: "A few customers told us they didn't want to automate because it might mean laying off data engineers. I respect that answer."
Inside: kind-leader view: automate the repetitive half (Fivetran 2026: 53% of engineering time on pipeline upkeep) so people move closer to the business; Gartner expects 75% of DE workflows automated by 2029, so plan the reskilling now.
Sources: Questions for Context; voice Q8; Fivetran 2026; Gartner via CIO&Leader (2026-09-24).

N2 [R or C | text + editorial image]
Hook: "We see two kinds of reaction when data engineers meet AI. We want to work with the second kind."
Inside: worry vs "how could this help me do more"; human judgement stays central; SO 2025: 84% use AI, only 33% trust its accuracy, so healthy doubt is fine and curiosity is the difference.
Sources: Richard "Data Engineers We Want" doc; Stack Overflow 2025.

N3 [S | carousel thought leadership]
Hook: "AI agents can now move data almost perfectly. Transforming it correctly is a different story."
Inside: benchmark honesty (ELT-Bench-Verified: load ~96%, transform ~33%; Spider 2.0 DBT best ~66%), so AI where useful, fixed rules and human review where it matters.
Sources: overview doc; arXiv ELT-Bench-Verified (confirm numbers); Spider 2.0 leaderboard.

N4 [S | single image worked example]
Hook: "A faster Snowflake warehouse that costs more per hour can still be the cheaper one. Or not. Here's the math."
Inside: rate is not cost, rate x time is. Gen2 burns 1.35x credits per hour on AWS/GCP, so it must finish ~26% faster to break even (20% on Azure); plus the 60-second minimum on every resume.
Sources: Snowflake Credit Consumption Table + docs.

N5 [R | text]
Hook: "I used to sell Talend. On 31 January 2024, the free version many teams started on was retired."
Inside: what happened and what users were told; the seller-chair lesson: the tool you start free on becomes the tool you can't leave, so know where your pipeline logic lives.
Sources: Richard doc; Qlik Talend Open Studio page.

N6 [C | carousel comparison]
Hook: "4 legacy data tools changed owner or status in the last 3 years. Is one of them running your pipelines?"
Inside: Informatica (Salesforce, Nov 2025; PowerCenter 10.5 standard support ended Mar 2026), Talend Open Studio (retired), StreamSets (IBM), Cloudera (CDH/HDP end of support). Three options each: pay for extended support, move to the vendor cloud, or move the logic to code you own.
Sources: Salesforce, Qlik, IBM, Cloudera primary pages; PowerCenter date to confirm.

Sources: overview doc; facts.md lock-in stance.
