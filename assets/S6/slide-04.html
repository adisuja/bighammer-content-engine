2. Time zones shift your dates.

Timestamps default to TIMESTAMP_LTZ, read in the session time zone.

one event, two sessions
event_ts = 2026-10-15 02:30 UTC

-- session time zone UTC
to_date(event_ts)  → 2026-10-15

-- session time zone America/New_York
to_date(event_ts)  → 2026-10-14

Same row, different day. Daily totals move and nobody gets an error.
