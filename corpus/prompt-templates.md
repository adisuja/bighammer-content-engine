## Post Structure Analysis

### 1. Opening Hook (5-10 words)

- Purpose: Capture attention with a universal truth or common desire

- Style: Simple, direct statement

- Format: Single line with line break

- Example from post: "Everyone's looking for their superpower."

Alternative approaches:

- Ask a thought-provoking question

- Share a surprising statistic

- Make a bold claim

### 2. Pattern Interrupt (2-5 words)

- Purpose: Create anticipation and break expected thought pattern

- Style: Conversational transition

- Format: Single line with line breaks before and after

- Example from post: "But here's what they miss:"

Alternative approaches:

- "Here's the truth:"

- "Plot twist:"

- "The reality?"

### 3. Core Message (3-5 words)

- Purpose: Deliver key insight with impact

- Style: Short, powerful statement

- Format: Single line with emphasis

- Example from post: "You already have it."

### 4. Misconception Framework (15-20 words)

- Purpose: Address common limiting beliefs

- Style: Bulleted list with parallel structure

- Format: Preceded by "It's not about:"

- Example from post:

  • Finding the right moment

  • Waiting for permission

  • Searching for validation

### 5. Bridge Statement (5-7 words)

- Purpose: Connect misconceptions to truth

- Style: Direct, empowering statement

- Format: Single line with line break

- Example from post: "Your potential is already there."

### 6. Pain Points (20-25 words)

- Purpose: Demonstrate understanding of audience struggles

- Style: Bulleted list introduced by observation

- Format: "I see too many people:" followed by bullets

- Example from post:

  • Doubting their abilities

  • Letting imposter syndrome win

  • Comparing their journey to everyone else

### 7. Authority Statement (5-10 words)

- Purpose: Establish credibility through experience

- Style: Personal insight introduction

- Format: Single line with colon

- Example from post: "Here's what I've learned building in public:"

### 8. Core Lessons (25-30 words)

- Purpose: Share key insights

- Style: Three parallel statements

- Format: Short, impactful lines with breaks

- Example from post:

  The most beautiful things start messy.

  The best projects begin imperfect.

  The greatest impact comes from starting small.

### 9. Closing Truth (10-15 words)

- Purpose: Deliver final powerful message

- Style: Two-part statement with line break

- Format: Set up and payoff

- Example from post:

  "You don't need to be perfect.

   You just need to begin."
