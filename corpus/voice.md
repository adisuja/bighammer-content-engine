Sources: Consolidated Document -> "Srinath - LinkedIn Voice & Content Discovery" (Srinath's own

Read 2026-09-30. These override anything softer in brand/tokens.json -> voice.

## Srinath Reddy - Founder & CEO, BigHammer.ai (primary author)
**Who he writes for:** CDO, CTO, SVP/VP Data Engineering; Architects and Directors of Data &
Analytics; data engineers. Goal of every post: they book a demo or reach out.
**Cadence he wants:** 2 posts/week - **1 leadership + 1 BigHammer/technical**.
**His pillars:** (1) Databricks AND Snowflake cost optimisation (2) consolidating legacy point
solutions (3) migrating data + legacy ETL code to public cloud (4) building data products with AI
agents from natural language / no-code (5) no vendor lock-in. Personal brand: visionary who
disrupts the status quo; a **kind** leader who invests in people and mentors junior talent.
**Avoid:** replacing or competing with public cloud providers.

How he sounds (his words):
- Story-first. Very casual. Very technical. Always on topic.
- Structure every time: **what is the problem -> how we fix it -> what is the result.**
- Very simple English. No words a normal person would not understand. No bad words. Always kind.
- Short punchy phrases, anecdotes, metaphors ("Don't use an F1 race track for a bicycle").
- Direct and prescriptive. "No generic marketing BS."
- Controversy is fine if it challenges a thought. **Never attack a person or a product.**
- Open to honest, personal posts (confession openers are OK).
- Length: half a page to one page. A LinkedIn post, not an article link.

Hard rules:
1. **No dashes ("-" as punctuation, em dashes) and no tick marks.** (So no check/cross lists.)
2. Nothing that reads like copy-pasted ChatGPT.
3. Emojis OK. Hashtags OK. **No client tagging / naming** (SBC Labs testimonial is public on the site).
4. **Every number needs approval from Varadha (Head of Engineering)** before publishing.
5. Always "**up to**" in front of TCO/savings claims ("up to ~50% lower Databricks TCO").
6. Don't say "the Databricks bill is impossible to predict" (legal risk). Say "your platform bill".
7. Images carry **"Srinath Reddy - Founder & CEO"** (never "Data Architect") and the BigHammer logo.
8. Migration story = move jobs **from Databricks to public cloud, then AI agents run and manage them.**
9. Do not re-post anything already published (check corpus/used-content-registry.md).

Useful true material: 20 years in data; Dun & Bradstreet and Aon; 600+ people across data
engineering, governance and data quality; 200K+ datasets; data used by banks worldwide; FTC
compliance SLAs; 1 AM / 4 AM calls; 30-minute CEO updates; long-term roadmap for "transformation &
modernization"; "high performance culture" where people are KIND to each other; 12-hour days
that felt energising. (Stories #1, #4, #6, #7, #8 in the rejected set already used several.)
